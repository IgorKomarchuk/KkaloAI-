Why GLP-1 Requires Specialized Tracking

Medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, and Mounjaro are revolutionizing weight loss, but they come with a high risk: Muscle Loss. When you don't eat enough because your appetite is suppressed, your body takes energy from muscle tissue first.

Important: Medical studies show that up to 40% of weight lost on GLP-1 can be lean muscle mass if nutrition isn't managed correctly.
