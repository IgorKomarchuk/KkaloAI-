One Source of Truth: Health Connect

Your nutrition doesn't exist in a vacuum. KkaloAI integrates deeply with Android Health Connect, pulling data from your smartwatch, smart scales, and fitness trackers into one unified dashboard.

Real-Time Budget Adjustments

Stop following static plans. KkaloAI adjusts your calorie and macro budget based on what happened in the last 24 hours:

  • 🏃 Active Burn Sync: If you went for a 5km run, KkaloAI automatically detects the burned energy and adjusts your protein and carb needs for recovery.
  • ⚖️ Weight Trend Logic: Seamlessly imports weight data to calculate your actual TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) over time.
  • 💤 Sleep-Nutrition Correlation: Your AI Coach analyzes how poor sleep affects your craving patterns, helping you prepare for difficult days.
