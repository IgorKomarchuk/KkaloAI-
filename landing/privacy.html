Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

1. Overview

KkaloAI ("we", "our", or "us") provides AI-powered nutritional tracking services. This policy outlines how we handle your data in compliance with Google Play Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

2. Data Collection & AI Analysis

We collect only the data necessary to provide personalized nutrition insights:

  • AI Photo Analysis: Food photos are processed in real-time by Groq API (using Meta Llama 4 Scout vision model). Photos are NOT stored on our servers and are discarded immediately after calories are calculated.
  • Nutritional Logs: Your meal history, calorie counts, and macro data are stored locally on your device or in our secure cloud if you use synchronization.
  • GLP-1 Companion Data: If enabled, optional symptom tracking (e.g., appetite, energy levels) is stored locally to provide personalized nutritional coaching. This data is NEVER shared with insurance companies or pharmaceutical third parties.

3. Health Connect Integration

KkaloAI participates in Android Health Connect. If you grant permission, we write and/or read nutrition data to help you maintain a holistic health profile. This data is handled according to the Google Play Health Connect Permissions Policy.

4. Data Minimization & Security

We use industry-standard TLS encryption for all data in transit. We prioritize local processing to keep your personal lifestyle choices private. We do not sell your data to advertisers.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to access, export, or delete your data at any time. You can clear your meal history directly within the app settings. For a full account deletion and metadata removal, please use the "Delete Account" button in settings or contact us.

6. Medical Disclaimer

KkaloAI is a nutritional information tool and is NOT a medical device. Our AI Coach and GLP-1 Companion provide general wellness information based on your logs. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider for any medical condition.
