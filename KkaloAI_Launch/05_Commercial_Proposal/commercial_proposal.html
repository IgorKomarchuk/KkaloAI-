Who we are

KkaloAI is a mobile-first AI nutrition platform founded in 2026, headquartered remotely with operations in Eastern Europe. Built by an indie product team with a track record in mobile growth, the product is engineered around one principle: the user always confirms.

Where competitors hide pricing behind onboarding, KkaloAI puts every tier on the home screen. Where they ship one-shot AI estimates, KkaloAI ships an adjustable slider. Where they ignore underserved health journeys, KkaloAI bakes them in as first-class modes.
